# Effects

Hit and spell effects (slash, claw, pierce, impact, crit, burst, fire, ice, lightning, poison, holy, shadow, nature, arcane, shatter, heal, level up, blood) were drawn for Solaria by tools/make_fx.gd. CC0.

No third-party effect art is used. Everything here is original.

2.1: tools/bake_art.gd mapped the effects to the Solaria palette and cut their soft alpha to a few hard steps (crisp pixels). The originals are in git history.

glow.png (the soft pool of light under torches, fires and door reads) and shadow.png (the blob under every sprite) were drawn for Solaria by tools/draw_hall_bits.py in the four alpha steps. CC0.
