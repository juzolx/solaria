# Room props and events

Every file here (chests, mimic, shrine altar, nest, campfire, camp, statues, fountains, braziers, bones, candles, mushrooms...) was made for Solaria with PixelLab (pixellab.ai, Juzo's subscription), then snapped to art/palette.gpl, given hard alpha and the one #0e0812 outline, 40x40 (ArtStyle.ROOM_PROP_SIZE).

Fable dungeon round 1: skull.png (3x), candles.png and bones.png (2x) were shrunk on their 40x40 canvases with the outline redrawn, so they match the one pixel size of the room.
