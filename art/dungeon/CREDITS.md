# Dungeon tiles

Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup 32x32 tiles, CC0 (https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles), by the DCSS artists (https://github.com/crawl/tiles/blob/master/ARTISTS.md). Copied by tools/dcss_dungeon.py.

pot.png, lever.png, nest.png and mimic.png were drawn for Solaria (same CC0 terms). altar.png, key.png and npc.png are DCSS tiles.

pillar.png and boulder.png were redrawn for Solaria in 2.1 by tools/bake_art.gd (same CC0 terms); the old DCSS pictures are in git history. trap_flames.png and trap_frost.png were swapped (the fire trap now shows the red ring, the frost trap the icy one).

2.1: tools/bake_art.gd mapped these files to the Solaria palette (art_style.gd, art/palette.gpl), made every pixel solid and gave them one dark outline (not the floor and wall tiles). The originals are in git history.
