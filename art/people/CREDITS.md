# People

Room people (the wounded stranger, the six travelers) and the eleven town keepers, made for Solaria with PixelLab (pixellab.ai, Juzo's subscription) in the base hero's style (the PixelLab hero as the style image), hard alpha, their own colours like the hero. 64x64. File names: travelers by their travelers.gd id, keepers by lore.gd KEEPER_ART.

smith.png (Gilda) was redrawn for Fable dungeon round 1 with a PixelLab text edit (edit_image_pixen) of the first version, so she no longer looks like Hilde the town smith: dark hair under a green hood, a pack and bedroll, the hammer on her belt.
