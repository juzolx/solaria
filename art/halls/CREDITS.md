# First-person room textures

Walls, floors and ceilings: Tiny Texture Packs 1, 2 and 3 by Screaming Brain Studios, CC0 (https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-texture-pack-1). Shrunk to 32x32, palette-cut and graded by tools/fp_textures.py (source unpacked at /home/claude/tex).

door.png and door_open.png were drawn for Solaria (CC0).
