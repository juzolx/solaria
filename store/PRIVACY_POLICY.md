# Solaria Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date of release]_

Solaria is an offline game. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device
- Your heroes, towns, items and settings are saved **only on your device**. The game also keeps a few backup copies of your save on the device so a damaged save can be restored. These never leave your phone.
- The game keeps a small **error log** on your device to help fix problems. It stays on the device. If you choose "Copy report info" in the menu, the text is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it into a message yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.
- If you make a **share card**, the picture is saved as a file on your device. It is only shared if you choose to share it yourself.
- You can remove all of this at any time by deleting the app (this removes the saves, log and cards from your device) or by deleting a hero in the game menu.

## What we do not do
- The game has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and it does not sell or share any data.
- The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.
- Nothing about you is sent to us or to any third party, so we hold no data about you that could be viewed or deleted.

## Purchases
This version of the game has no purchases.

## Children
Solaria does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes (for example if online features are added in a future version), the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the game's App Privacy details on the App Store will be updated to match.
