# Solaria Privacy Policy (DRAFT for Juzo to check before publishing)

_Last updated: [date of release]_

Solaria is a game you can play fully offline. This page explains what information the game uses.

## On your device
- Your heroes, towns, items and settings are saved **only on your device**. The game keeps a few backup copies of your save on the device so a damaged save can be restored. These never leave your phone.
- The game has **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and it does not sell or share any data.

## Online features (optional)
Online features (leaderboards, guilds, raids, cloud backup) are optional. Only if you choose to use them:
- The game creates an **anonymous account** with our online service provider, **Supabase** (supabase.com), which stores the data below for us. The account is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number.
- We store the **online name you pick**, your best floors and runs for the leaderboards, your guild membership and raid scores, and any **cloud backup** you choose to make.
- Other players can see your online name, your leaderboard runs and your guild.
- You can **delete your online account at any time** in the game (Menu > Cloud backup > Delete my online account). This removes everything above from our server right away.
- If you never turn online features on, nothing is sent anywhere.

## Purchases
Purchases (if offered) are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The game only remembers which items you own.

## Children
Solaria does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
