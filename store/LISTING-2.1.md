## Description (about 1,500 chars)
The world is dying. Nobody knows why. The walled towns are the last safe ground, and the only hope left is the dungeon beneath them.

Solaria is a dark fantasy dungeon crawler for one hand and short breaks. Lead a team of heroes through endless floors. Pick a door, face what waits, and decide when to turn back. What you carry is only safe once you are home.

- Extraction: every floor pays more, every fall costs more
- Evolve your class at levels 15, 35 and 75, and chase secret and God classes
- 12 races and over 100 monsters from folklore, plus 10 Calamities that shake the deep
- Gear with Gear Scores, sets and uniques; Cursed loot you cleanse at the Church
- Nests hide rare pet eggs, one kind for each region
- Build your town: Inn, Smith, Church, Vault and more, each one levels up
- Up to three towns, each with its own dungeon and heroes
- Regular towns: the goddess wakes the fallen. Hardcore towns: there is no goddess to save you
- Fortune's Wheel at the Tavern, played with game gold only
- Autoplay with rules you set, or fight every blow yourself
- Portrait, one-handed, built for the phone
- No ads. No energy timers. Nothing advances while the game is closed.

Go down, and be let out. (about 1,350 chars)
