## Description
Solaria is a dark fantasy dungeon crawler built for one hand and short breaks.

Lead a team of up to three heroes down through endless floors of moss-choked hollows, flooded catacombs and ember halls. Choose a door, face what waits behind it, and decide when to turn back: the gold and gear you carry are only safe once you're home.

- Over 100 classes on branching paths, from Warrior to god-rank legends
- 12 races, each remembered by the peoples of the deep
- More than 100 monsters from folklore, rare wanderers, and Calamities that shake the whole dungeon
- Loot with Gear Scores, sets, uniques and a monster's own Signature item
- Pets to find, hatch and raise (they're rare: treasure them)
- Build your town: up to three towns, each with its own roster of heroes
- Autoplay with rules you set, or fight every blow yourself
- No ads. No energy timers. Nothing advances while the game is closed.
