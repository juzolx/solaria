## Juzo's decisions (11:06 PM)
- **Fights are back-to-front, Pokémon style:** your team seen from behind at the bottom of the screen, monsters facing you at the top. Reason: three heroes, pets and summons (necromancers etc.) crowd a side view fast.
- **One room per floor.** Clear the room, pick one of three blank doors, and that's the next floor.

## More decisions (2:04-2:12 AM, before Juzo went to sleep)
- **This update grows:** room interactions, hidden dungeon portals, dungeon towns + fast travel, and egg quests are all in it (stages 3 and 4 fold into this update). Version **0.13 "The Halls Below"**.
- **Install each finished part as you go** (Juzo, 2:18 AM, replaces "don't install"): so if usage runs out he can play the latest working version and give tips (he's back Sunday). Use tools/pc_hash.py on the PC and tools/make_install_zip.py here.
- **Rooms:** you see the monsters standing in the room first and tap one to start that fight (you choose the order; sneaky classes can sometimes slip past). Autoplay fights them in order, picks doors at random and skips interactions.
- **Fights:** usually one monster, sometimes packs of 2-3 (wolves, imps); bosses can bring minions. Back-to-front view.
- **Chests:** about 1 in 8 is a mimic (tougher, better loot); classes with sight can spot them.
- **Traps:** damage (spikes, darts, fire jets), status effects (poison, slow, blind, curse) and ambush alarms (extra monsters). No gold-stealing or gear-breaking traps.
- **Portals:** about one every 100 floors (once per region on average). Inside, you can only leave early with a Return Scroll. 10 floors, guaranteed gold, better gear, a clear bonus; a theme per portal.
- **Dungeon towns:** one per 100-floor region, run by a race that fits. Hidden entrance (looks like a normal door until found), then named and a fast-travel point: start dives from it like a checkpoint. In town: shop and rest, bank carried gold, NPC quests (friendliness; some end in eggs), secrets by alignment. Hated by the town's race: guards turn you away.
- **Door reading:** specific classes, mostly the Ranger line. A reader levels up: at first doors glow green / yellow / red (safe / unsure / danger); leveled up, they tell you what's behind.
- **Egg quests:** from dungeon town NPCs, room events (a dying beast's young, a nest behind a door), and rare Bounty Board nest contracts.
- **Mood by region:** forest mist and fireflies; barrows dark with bones and torchlight; coast dripping water; fire lands embers; myths shadows; realms keep their own look. Deeper = darker everywhere.
