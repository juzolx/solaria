# Fable review: HERO ART, round 1 of 5 (Oct 25 2026)

Scope: the PixelLab hero (art/heroes, hero_art.gd, sprites.gd, battle_view.gd, character_creator.gd, races.gd) and the 14 races in game.
Evidence: the heroes_ingame pictures, pixel measurements of the baked sheets (bounding box per 96-px cell, colour counts, connected components), and the code on big-update (wt/heroes is merged: f69db347 only adds HeroArt.steps()).
Not reviewed in depth: fairy wings (petal wings in progress; note that fairy_m/race_fairy.png is currently EMPTY in every cell, so fairies ship with no wings until they land).

Frame cells are 96x96, foot row 80; "bbox x0,y0-x1,y1" is the used rect inside one cell. Frames: 0-1 idle, 2-5 attack (atk0-atk3), 6 hit, 7-8 portrait.

## The list (worst first)

1. **Long weapons are clipped by the 96-px cell in battle** - where: art/heroes/m/weapon_staff.png atk0 bbox 66,0-95,50 (cut at the top AND right edge), atk2/atk3 0,57-31,75 (cut at the left edge); m/weapon_bow atk2 0,49-31,66; m/weapon_spear idle0 64,61-95,77, hit 63,63-95,72 (the spear tip is cut flat at col 95 in the pose you see most), atk2 0,57-29,68; f/weapon_staff atk2 0,52-31,95 (cut left AND bottom); dwarf_m/weapon_bow atk3 13,46-33,85 (5 rows under the ground line). Seen in 5_heroes_frames row 1 cols 5-6 and row 4 cols 5-6 (the staff/bow end flat at the picture edge). At 3x a flat cut edge is 1 screen pixel from obvious. What's wrong: weapons are baked per frame into sheets as PixelLab drew them, with no room in the cell. Fix: stop baking weapons; keep ONE drawing per weapon (heroes.json "weapons") with a pivot, and place it in code per frame at the hand point already in heroes.json "hand" (per body/build/frame) with a per-frame angle table; draw it on its own layer so it can leave the 96 cell (battle_view already does this for the old hero: _draw_hero's draw_set_transform at the hand). If baking stays, bake weapons on a 128-wide cell and keep foot=80.

2. **The Orc mage's staff drops to the floor during the attack** - where: art/heroes/orc_m/weapon_staff.png atk0 bbox 61,65-95,95, atk2 59,63-86,95, atk3 59,63-91,95 (the human staff in the same frames is 66,0-95,50 and 0,57-31,75). Picture: 15_orc_fixed row 2 col 3 (staff hanging bottom-right, crystal cut by the edge). The staff leaves the hand in 3 of 4 swing frames and is cut at the cell bottom. Same family of bug: goblin_m/weapon_staff portrait idle1 bbox 39,59-78,95 (the staff lies at the feet in the second portrait frame; latent today because menus only show frame 7). Fix: as item 1 (code placement), or re-bake orc_m and goblin_m staff from the human frames mirrored to the orc hand table.

3. **The swing has no middle: atk2 and atk3 are duplicates, so the blade teleports** - where: m/outfit_warrior atk2 32,45-59,79 vs atk3 32,44-59,79; m/weapon_sword atk2 16,56-31,62 vs atk3 15,57-31,64; staff atk2 0,57-31,75 vs atk3 0,58-31,77 (same on f and the builds). atk1 has the weapon high right (rows 0-41), atk2 has it low left (rows 56-75): at 9 fps there is no frame in between. Picture: 5_heroes_frames row 2 cols 4-6. Fix: make atk2 the diagonal mid-swing (weapon across the body, tip at about the shoulder line) and keep atk3 as the low-left follow-through; or in code, tween the weapon angle between the atk1 and atk3 hand points over the two frames (needs item 1).

4. **The class picker does not read as 13 classes** - where: 3_class_picker.png; class_tab.gd:178 outfit_stage(scale := 2) and class_tab.gd:64 (2x), and the stage shows the EQUIPPED weapon (sword) on every card. At 2x on a phone Warrior/Knight/Wanderer/Cleric are the same brown-haired kid with a stick; only the robe colour differs. Fix: cards at 3x (the bible's one scale), each card holds the class family's signature weapon (plate: sword/axe, robe: staff, leather: bow/dagger, cloth: fist/spear) regardless of what is equipped, and the card frame takes the class colour. Keep the hero's own race/hair so it still feels like "you".

5. **Fallen Angel and the awakening looks have no art** - where: hero_art.gd:270-274 art_race() only maps a form that exists in heroes.json "builds"/"races"; "Fallen Angel" is in neither, so a fallen hero keeps the white wings and halo (6_races_standard_body row 3) while races.gd:184-186 promises "Black wings, a cracked halo". hero_art.gd never reads app["look"], so Archangel "A second pair of wings" (races.gd:77), Seraph "A third pair" (:78), Demon Noble "Great horns" (:88), Undead "glow" (:137) all show nothing (the old race_look() layer lists in sprites.gd:241-274 are computed and then ignored by _hero_pl). Fix now: a m/f race_fallen_angel layer = the angel wings recoloured dark (#2a2230 ramp) + a broken halo (cheap bake), and a wings2 layer for Archangel/Seraph; register them in heroes.json "races". Until drawn, at least tint the angel wings in _lay for form == "Fallen Angel" so the promise is not a lie.

6. **Builds lose the creator: one face, one hair, and dead skin swatches** - where: character_creator.gd:158 hides "hair" and "face" for Goblin, Dwarf, Orc, Werewolf, Fairy; heroes.json builds Orc/Goblin "skin": false yet races.gd:17 (Orc skins [6,3,4,7]) and :29 (Goblin [6,2,3]) make character_creator.gd:166 show 3-4 swatches that change nothing. Every goblin hero is the same goblin (7_goblin_build: one head per body). The fairy is a human-shaped head with no hair choice. Fix now: hide the skin row when info()["builds"][race]["skin"] is false. Next: bake hair layers for the builds in the same way as m/f (fairy first - its head is human-scaled; dwarf/goblin/orc need the 37 styles re-fitted to the head only, a cheap PixelLab per-frame edit).

7. **The dyed build outfits are a different palette from the drawn ones** - where: hero_art.gd:262-266 _dye_ramp uses v = [0.16..0.66] of the UI class colour (Mage #8b5cf6, v 0.96). The lightest dye band is the largest band in the robe (goblin_m/outfit_robe idle0: index 255 = 316 px of 546 dyed px) and lands at v 0.63, s 0.82; the drawn human mage robe's main colour is #512865 (v 0.40, s 0.60). The same class is a deep muted purple on humans (6_races row 2) and a bright violet on goblins/dwarves/orcs/werewolves/fairies (7/8/15/14/12 row 2). Also the dyed robes have no hue shift in the shadows (the bible's cooler/purple shadows). Fix: derive the dye from the drawn outfit, not the UI colour: per class, take the dominant colour of m/outfit_<class> frame 0 as the mid shade and build the 5-ramp around it with a hue shift (-15 deg, +sat in the shadow). Quick fix if time is short: v = [0.10,0.17,0.25,0.34,0.44], s * 0.7.

8. **Four hero scales across screens** - where: battle = _unit() (3 pt per art px); hero_tab.gd:96 = 3x; class_tab.gd:64, :178 and race_tab.gd:50 = 2x; party_tab.gd:114 and hero_select.gd:75 = 1x (64 pt). 4_party.png shows the leader as a 45-px thumbnail two taps from a 165-px Hero page. The bible: ONE integer scale, 3x on phones. Fix: 3x wherever the hero is the subject (party leader card, hero select, class cards), 2x only inside lists of many heroes, never 1x.

9. **The werewolf's tail goes through the floor** - where: orc_m/race_werewolf idle0 bbox 23,12-72,84 and orc_f 24,13-71,83 while the body's foot row is 79 (every outfit ends at row 79). Picture: 14_werewolf_grey row 1 col 1 and col 6: the tail tip is 4-5 art px (12-15 screen px) under the feet and the shadow ellipse. Fix: curl the tail up so it ends at row 78 (also fixes the hit frame 24,19-71,83).

10. **The menu portrait crop cuts the staff crystal and the spear tip** - where: sprites.gd:28 PORTRAIT_CROP = (16,24,64,64) on a canvas where the cell lands at (0,8) (sprites.gd:398), so a portrait shows cell rows 16-79 and cols 16-79 only. m/weapon_staff portrait idle0 bbox 16,9-39,64: rows 9-15 (the crystal) are cut; m/weapon_spear portrait 4,63-34,80: cols 4-15 (the tip) and row 80 are cut; hair_very_long portrait 27,25-69,81: rows 80-81 cut; the feet row 79 sits on the very last crop row (2_hero_page: the boots touch the picture edge, the shadow is drawn by the page). Fix: a taller crop: cell rows 4-83 x cols 8-87 (80x80) shown at 3x = 240 pt (hero_tab's 3x box is 192 - grow it; Sprites.PORTRAIT becomes 80). Cheapest: shift the crop to (16,16,64,64) in canvas space so the crystal keeps 6 rows and the feet get 8 rows of margin.

11. **Dark-on-dark: the hero from behind is a black shape on a black floor** - where: m/outfit_warrior idle0: 55% of its pixels are below luminance 40 (near black); m/hair_very_long back: 34%; the hair ramp 0 in art_info.gd "hairs"[0] = #717171 #574e54 #3a2e39 #0d0a0e #0c0a0e has two near-black shades for 2 of 5 steps. 1_fight.png: the hero is hair + outline on a dark floor, lit only by the 0.42-alpha top rim (battle_view.gd:1946). Juzo's monster rule ("no dark-on-dark, lifted mid-tones") applies to the hero too. Fix: in art_info "hairs", floor the darkest two steps at about #241a28 / #2e2433 for every dark ramp; raise the rim light to 0.6 and add the same 1-px rim on both sides (two more draw_texture_rect of the white at +-u); consider a faint ground glow under the hero like the monsters' plate light.

12. **The HP plate is drawn over the boots** - where: 1_fight.png, the "367 / 989" plate's top edge covers the bottom of both boots (y 578-583 on the 430x932 shot). Fix: place the plate top at foot row + 2 art px (below _hero_rect().end.y), not inside the hero rect.

13. **Stray single pixels** - where: m/hair_very_long portrait frame 7 and f/hair_very_long frame 7: 4 one-pixel islands each (components of size 1,1,1,1,6 next to the 888-px hair); m/hair_very_long back frame 0: islands of 1 and 2; orc_m/race_werewolf back frame 0: 6 islands <= 2 px; m/race_undead back frame 0: 5; m/outfit_warrior frame 7: 2. 14. **The Orc head breaks the chibi rule** - where: orc_m/outfit_plate idle0 row widths: head rows 13-30 are 4-26 px wide (about 16 px at the ears) on a body 46-50 px wide, head height 17 of 67 rows = 25%; the human warrior head is 24-28 px on a 28-px body, 46% of the height (hair 27 of 59 rows). Bible: big head ~40-43%. Picture: 15_orc_fixed row 1 col 1 (a pea on a boulder). 15. **The Undead signature is a glowing spine drawn on top of the coat** - where: 6_races_standard_body row 7 cols 1 and 4 (m/race_undead: a green vertebra column over the Conjurer coat in the back view and over the chest in front). Anatomy: a spine is inside the body; on clothes it reads as a glowing caterpillar. 16. **The female idle pops 2 px** - where: f/outfit_warrior idle0 bbox 33,45-63,79 vs idle1 35,43-62,79 (the male is 45/45): at 2 fps the whole torso jumps 2 art px up and 2 right, 6 screen px, every half second. Fix: in bake.py align idle1 to idle0 (translate 0,+2 / -2,0) or redraw idle1 as a 1-px shoulder breath only.

17. **Hero build cost: a GDScript per-pixel loop over the whole sheet, re-decoding every time** - where: hero_art.gd:88-127 _lay walks 96 rows x 672 columns = 64,512 px per layer for the battle (the hero uses about 30x60 of each cell, ~20%), 5-7 layers per hero, about 300-400k iterations with 5-6 PackedByteArray reads each; the Class tab builds 13 portraits (about 1.2M iterations); hero_art.gd:216 _imgs.clear() after EVERY build, so each of those 13 builds re-loads and re-decodes 5-6 RGBA sheets; _built (hero_art.gd:28) is never evicted and HeroArt.clear_cache (:301) is called from nowhere. Estimate on an A15 in GDScript: 0.3-0.6 s per hero, 1-2 s to open the Class tab the first time. Fix: in _lay, restrict x/y to the cell's used rect (precompute get_used_rect per cell once per sheet and store it in _imgs); keep _imgs until the caller says a batch is done (clear in clear_cache and on scene change, not per build); cap _built with an LRU of ~24 looks; build class cards one per frame (await) so the tab opens instantly.

18. **Sprites' hero cache key includes every armour piece although armour no longer changes the look** - where: sprites.gd:301 hero() key = str(app)+str(looks) and :459 hero_back() likewise; looks carries gear_look dicts for Chest/Legs/Feet/Head/Off Hand (rarity, trim colours), so each armour swap makes a new entry with 3 new ImageTextures and a silhouette, and hero_tab.gd:331 re-fetches the portrait on any gear change. Fix: when HeroArt.available(), key on (app, Class, Weapon type, glow) only.

19. **The idle sword is unreadable from behind** - where: m/weapon_sword idle0 bbox 63,62-75,75 (13x14 px, the grip inside the hip silhouette 32-63); 1_fight.png shows a grey smudge at the right hip, nothing that says "sword" (the Warrior that Juzo rated 10/10 was judged on the sheet, not at phone size over a dark floor). Fix: in idle/hit hold the blade out and angled (tip 6+ px clear of the body, like the old 2.0 hero's upright weapon), which code placement (item 1) makes a one-number change.

20. **Portrait frame 8 (s_idle1) is never shown** - where: hero_art.gd:228 anims(back=false) returns the 2 portrait frames, but every menu uses Sprites.portrait() (sprites.gd:524) = frame 7 only; nothing draws the front "combat_idle". So half the portrait art (and its bugs, item 2 goblin staff) is dead weight: 2 of 9 cells, the face layers' second cell, and a quarter of the portrait build time. Fix: either animate the Hero page portrait (2 fps, 2 frames) so the centrepiece breathes, or drop frame 8 from the bake and the loops.

## Score: 6 / 10

The base hero and the portrait face are genuinely good at phone size (2_hero_page), the 13 standard-body outfits read as different clothes, and the race signatures (elf, angel, demon, dragonkin, beastkin, lich) land. What pulls it down is everything around the weapon: clipped and dropped weapons in the swing (1-3), a class picker where classes are invisible (4), promised forms that never show (5), and the five build races being one fixed person in a brighter palette (6-7).

## Biggest opportunity

Place ONE drawing per weapon in code at the per-frame hand points that heroes.json already holds, instead of baking seven weapon sheets per body build. That single change fixes the clipping (1), the orc staff (2), gives the swing a tweened middle (3), lets the class cards hold the class weapon (4), makes the idle sword readable (19), and cuts 7 sheets x 10 builds of art that must be re-baked whenever a frame changes - which is exactly the "weapons change the look, armour doesn't" rule, done in the cheapest place.
