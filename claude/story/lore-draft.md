## 1. World summary
The world is dying, and nobody knows why. Wells go quiet, rivers wander, and the sun
hangs black over everything. The last safe places are walled towns, and each one sits
over a dungeon that opened in the earth. Some dungeons spread across the land; some
never did. The people believe the way out is down: a carving at the entrance says so,
the first descenders never came back, and strange light and wind rise from below. So
every town sends its heroes down, because it is the only thing left to try. [new: wells
failing; the same carving at every entrance]

## 2. Lore pages (13, all [new]; same format as lore.gd PAGES)

1. **The Walls** [new]. The walls went up before the first winter of the Eclipse. No one remembers who gave the order. Everyone remembers who was left outside.
2. **Safe Ground** [new]. Nothing hunts inside the walls. Children ask why. The old answer was "the goddess". The new answer is a shrug.
3. **The Carving** [new]. It is cut into the stone at every entrance, in the same hand. Weathered, but never worn. Each town reads it a little differently.
4. **The First Ones** [new]. The first descenders went deep and did not return. Some say they died. The town says they got out. The town needs that to be true.
5. **What Rises** [new]. Warm wind from below, on the coldest nights. A light no one can name. Once, a boot of a kind no town makes. People keep these things.
6. **The Dungeons That Did Not Spread** [new]. Dungeons opened all over the world. Some crept outward, swallowing roads. Some stayed small. No one can say what made the difference.
7. **The Dying** [new]. It is slow, so nobody can date it. Maps are out of date. Rivers have moved. The old say it began long before the Eclipse.
8. **Other Towns** [new]. Lanterns on far ridges, once. Fewer each year. Each town believes it is the one that will find the way.
9. **The Goddess** [new]. She is in every prayer and in no portrait. Her face is always turned away. Priests say it is modesty. Children say she is looking for something.
10. **Where the Fallen Wake** [new]. A hero who dies in a town of the faith wakes on the Church floor, cold and breathing. The priests never say why. They only say she is kind.
11. **No Goddess Here** [new]. Some towns lost the faith, or never had it. Their dead stay dead, and the graveyard grows. They descend anyway.
12. **Ascended** [new]. A rumor with no proof: a place above the dying world, for those who make it out. Nobody has described it. Everyone describes it differently.
13. **The Only Thing Left** [new]. Ask a descender why, and the honest ones say it plainly: there is nothing else. Then they pick up the lantern.

## 3. Death-screen lines (6 each) [new]

2. "The goddess is not done with you."

Hardcore (no goddess; final, quiet)

2. "No bell. No light. Only the dark you chose."
3. "They will bury you by the wall."
4. "Your name goes in the stone."
5. "The graveyard grows by one."
6. "Nobody is coming. Nobody was ever coming."

## 4. Church lines (4 each) [new]

Regular, holy
1. "Kneel, child. She hears even the quiet ones."
2. "Whoever you were down there, she carried you home."
3. "Bring me the cursed things. She forgives what she can."
4. "Light a candle. It costs little and means much."

Hardcore, abandoned (mechanics unchanged; only the voice)

2. "A cracked bowl. Dust where the candles were."
3. "Someone still scrubs the floor here. No one will say who."
4. "Cursed things are cleansed here by hands, not by grace. It takes longer."

## 5. The prophecy carving [new]
Cut at every dungeon entrance, same words, same hand:

> WHAT WAS TAKEN DOWN
> MAY BE CARRIED UP.
> THE DEEP REMEMBERS THE LIGHT.
> GO DOWN, AND BE LET OUT.

Short form (loading tip): "Go down, and be let out."
"Be let out" never says by whom.

## 7. One line per zone (10) [new]
- **Mossy Hollows**: The first roots reached here when the sun failed; even the moss is starving.
- **Sunken Catacombs**: Everyone who died before the dying is still down here, waiting.
- **Flooded Depths**: The sea rises from below while the wells above run dry. Nobody explains it.
- **Ember Halls**: A fire without a sun, burning what little the world has left.
- **The Shadow Deep**: The dark has weight here, as if the world were leaning into it.
- **The Hushwood** (elf): The last forest, hushed because it is listening.
- **The Glimmer Court** (fairy): Bright, polite and afraid; the Court counts its dwindling light.
- **The Ironroot Deeps** (dwarf): The dwarves forge against the cold and won't say how long the coal lasts.
- **The Gilded Spire** (angel): Gold and silence. The angels do not say whether they know.
- **The Cinder Abyss** (demon): The demons say the world is only finishing what it began.

## 8. Cursed loot and Calamities, tied to the dying world [new]
Cursed loot (the Church cleanses it):
- Curses are the dying world leaking into things: items that "caught" it below. A cleansed item is one the world has let go of, for now.
- Fits the mechanics: the downside (frail, brittle, dull) is what the dying takes; the hidden upside is what the item was before.
- Church voice: Regular priests say "she forgives it"; Hardcore only scrubs.

The 10 Calamities as symptoms (keep the existing lore lines; these are the hidden reading):
1. The Hollow Saint: faith with nothing behind it.
2. Ashmaw: the hunger of a world with too little left.
3. Nidhogg: the roots giving way.
4. The Pale Tide: water leaving, water returning.
5. Morrowgrave: the future arriving dead.
6. The Thousand-Eyed: the world watching its own end.
7. Skorvald: the cold that will not lift.
8. The Weeping Colossus: a guardian grieving what it failed to guard.
9. Vhal'gor: the sky emptying, one star at a time.
10. The Last Night: the end itself, walking.
